About

A multidisciplinary perspective on corporate law and finance.

CS Ummay Rabab Oruba is a Practising Company Secretary with expertise in Company Law, Corporate Governance, Banking, Finance, Securities Law and Insurance. Her work is grounded in outstanding performance in the Company Secretary examinations conducted by the Institute of Company Secretaries of India (ICSI), where she secured All India Ranks at all three levels.

An alumna of ICFAI Foundation for Higher Education, Hyderabad, she holds a Master of Laws (LL.M.) specialising in Banking, Corporate, Finance and Securities Law, and is currently pursuing a Diploma in Education in Insurance from the Insurance Institute of India.

Combining academic excellence, legal expertise, analytical ability and financial knowledge, she brings a valuable perspective to corporate governance, legal compliance, regulatory affairs and financial services. Committed to continuous learning, she aspires to make a meaningful contribution to the corporate and legal landscape.
